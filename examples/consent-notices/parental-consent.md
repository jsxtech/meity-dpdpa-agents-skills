WHAT PERSONAL DATA WE COLLECT
- Child's name and age
- Parent/guardian contact details (email, phone)
- App usage data (features used, time spent)
- Content preferences

SECTION 9 RESTRICTIONS — WHAT WE DO NOT DO
- We do NOT track, profile, or behaviourally monitor children
- We do NOT serve targeted advertising to children
- We do NOT process children's data in any way likely to cause harm

PURPOSE OF PROCESSING
1. Account creation — to set up a child-safe user profile
2. Age-appropriate content — to filter content for the child's age group
3. Educational features — learning tools, quizzes, progress tracking

We do not share your child's data with third parties except as required by law.

YOUR RIGHTS AS A DATA PRINCIPAL (Section 11–14)
As parent/guardian, you may:
- Access a summary of your child's personal data
- Correct or update inaccurate data
- Erase your child's data and delete the account
- Withdraw consent at any time
- Nominate another person to exercise these rights

COMPLAINT TO THE DATA PROTECTION BOARD OF INDIA
If unsatisfied, file a complaint with the DPBI at https://www.dpbi.gov.in
or as notified by the Central Government.
