# Consent Notice — Employee HR Data

```
CONSENT NOTICE
[Organisation Name] — Human Resources
Effective Date: [Date]

This notice explains how we handle your data as an employee under the
Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

PROCESSING WITHOUT CONSENT (Legitimate Use — Section 7(f))
The following does NOT require your consent:
- Payroll and salary disbursement
- Tax deductions and statutory filings (PF, ESI, gratuity)
- Statutory compliance and government reporting
- Performance management and appraisals

PROCESSING THAT REQUIRES YOUR CONSENT
Data collected: photographs, health/wellness info, survey responses, benefits
enrolment details.

Purposes:
1. Voluntary benefits — optional insurance, gym memberships, other schemes
2. Wellness programmes — health check-ups, mental health support, fitness
3. Employee surveys — feedback and engagement responses
4. Photo for ID/directory — photograph on ID cards and staff directory

YOUR RIGHTS AS A DATA PRINCIPAL (Section 11–14)
You have the right to:
- Access a summary of your personal data
- Correct or update inaccurate or incomplete data
- Erase personal data processed on the basis of consent
- Withdraw consent at any time by writing to HR or the Grievance Officer
- Nominate another person to exercise your rights

COMPLAINT TO THE DATA PROTECTION BOARD OF INDIA
If unsatisfied, file a complaint with the DPBI at https://www.dpbi.gov.in
or as notified by the Central Government.

by applicable labour and tax laws. Consent-based data (photos, wellness records,
survey responses) will be deleted within [X] days of separation unless you
request earlier deletion.

YOUR DUTIES AS A DATA PRINCIPAL (Section 15)
You must provide authentic and accurate information when sharing personal data.
```
