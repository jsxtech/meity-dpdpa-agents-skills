# Consent Notice — Health Data Collection

```
CONSENT NOTICE
[Organisation Name]
Effective Date: [Date]

Your health data is sensitive. Please read how we handle it under the Digital
Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

WHAT PERSONAL DATA WE COLLECT
- Full name and date of birth
- Contact details (phone, email, address)
- Medical history and health records
- Prescriptions and medication details
- Diagnostic test results (lab reports, imaging)
- Health insurance policy and claim information

PURPOSE OF PROCESSING
1. Treatment — medical consultation, diagnosis, and care
2. Diagnosis — ordering and interpreting tests and clinical assessments
3. Insurance claims — processing and settling claims with your insurer
4. Health records — maintaining records for continuity of care

We will NOT share your health data with any third party except treating doctors,
diagnostic labs, and your insurer as needed, unless required by law.

YOUR RIGHTS AS A DATA PRINCIPAL (Section 11–14)
You have the right to:
- Access a summary of your health data and processing activities
- Correct or update inaccurate or incomplete records
- Erase your data (subject to medical record retention laws)
- Withdraw consent at any time; this will not affect treatment already provided
- Nominate another person to exercise your rights on your behalf

To exercise any right, contact our Grievance Officer below.

COMPLAINT TO THE DATA PROTECTION BOARD OF INDIA
If unsatisfied with our response, file a complaint with the DPBI at
https://www.dpbi.gov.in or as notified by the Central Government.

GRIEVANCE OFFICER / DATA PROTECTION OFFICER
Name: [DPO/Grievance Officer Name]

DATA RETENTION (Section 8(7))
Health records are retained for the duration required by applicable medical
record retention laws and clinical standards. After this period, records will
be securely deleted or anonymised.

YOUR DUTIES AS A DATA PRINCIPAL (Section 15)
You must provide authentic and accurate information when sharing personal data.
Filing false or frivolous complaints may attract a penalty of up to ₹10,000.
```
