# DPDP Penalty Exposure Calculator

An estimation worksheet to help organisations understand their potential penalty exposure under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

## Instructions

1. List your processing activities
2. Map each activity to the applicable violation category
3. Apply aggravating and mitigating factors
4. Estimate your penalty exposure range

## Step 3: Aggravating Factors

Check all that apply — these increase exposure.

- [ ] Repeat violation

## Step 4: Mitigating Factors

Check all that apply — these reduce exposure.

- [ ] Voluntary disclosure of violation
- [ ] Immediate remediation steps taken
- [ ] Full cooperation with DPBI
- [ ] First offence
- [ ] Small-scale processing
- [ ] Robust compliance programme in place
- [ ] Prompt notification to affected Data Principals

---

## Step 5: Exposure Calculation

1. **Base Exposure** = Sum of statutory maximums for all applicable violation categories
2. **Adjustment Factor** = 0.1 to 1.0 (based on balance of aggravating vs mitigating factors)
   - Mostly mitigating → 0.1–0.3
   - Balanced → 0.4–0.6
   - Mostly aggravating → 0.7–1.0
3. **Adjusted Exposure** = Base × Adjustment Factor
4. **Estimated Range** = Adjusted × 0.5 to Adjusted × 1.0
