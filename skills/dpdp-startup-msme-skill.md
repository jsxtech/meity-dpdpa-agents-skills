# DPDP for Startups & MSMEs Skill

## Skill Identity

**Skill Name:** dpdp-startup-msme
**Domain:** Lean Compliance, Resource-Constrained Implementation, Startup/MSME Readiness
**Skill Type:** Operational, Strategic
**Applicable To:** Founders, Startup CTOs, MSME owners, Fractional DPOs

---

## Skill Purpose

Provide a **pragmatic, resource-proportionate path to DPDP compliance** for startups and micro/small/medium enterprises (MSMEs). It prioritises the highest-risk obligations, offers lightweight templates, and tracks the **pending Rule 23 exemption** for startups/small entities — while assuming full obligations until any exemption is notified.

> **Rule 23 watch:** The DPDP Rules 2025 delegate startup/small-entity exemptions to a future Central Government notification (Rule 23). **No exemptions are in force.** Assume full obligations; monitor via the Regulatory Monitoring Agent.

---

## Skill Capabilities

---

### Capability 1: Minimum Viable Compliance

Identify the smallest set of controls that address the highest-penalty obligations first: security safeguards, breach notification, consent/notice, and grievance.

---

### Capability 2: Lean Consent & Notice

Provide lightweight but compliant consent-notice templates suitable for early-stage products, aligned to Schedule II.

---

### Capability 3: Founder-as-DPO Interim Model

Guidance for assigning privacy accountability before a dedicated DPO is affordable, including a fractional/virtual DPO option.

---

### Capability 4: Low-Cost Security Baseline

A proportionate security-safeguards baseline (access control, encryption, backups, patching) mapped to Section 8(5), avoiding over-engineering.

---

### Capability 5: Phased Roadmap for Growth

A staged plan that scales controls as the startup grows (seed → growth → scale), linking to the Compliance Roadmap Agent.

---

### Capability 6: Exemption & Threshold Watch

Track the pending Rule 23 exemption and SDF thresholds (Rule 12) so the entity knows when heavier obligations may apply.

---

### Capability 7: Investor & Due-Diligence Readiness

Prepare the minimum privacy artefacts investors and enterprise customers expect (privacy policy, DPA, RoPA-lite, breach plan).

## Skill Guardrails

- **Never assume** a startup exemption exists — none is notified under Rule 23.
- **Always prioritise** the highest-penalty obligations first (security, breach, children).
- **Never skip** breach-notification readiness, however small the entity.
- **Always revisit** obligations at SDF thresholds as the entity scales.
