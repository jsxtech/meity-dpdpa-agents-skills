## Score Interpretation & Recommended Actions

### 96–120: Strong Compliance

Maintain your programme. Focus on continuous improvement and monitoring.

- Use the [Audit Compliance Agent](agents/dpdp-audit-compliance-agent.md) for ongoing oversight
- Use the [Audit Checklist Skill](skills/dpdp-audit-checklist-skill.md) for periodic reviews

### 60–95: Moderate Compliance

Prioritise identified gaps. Build out missing controls.

- Start with the [Compliance Roadmap Agent](agents/dpdp-compliance-roadmap-agent.md) for a phased plan
- Use the [Privacy Risk Management Skill](skills/dpdp-privacy-risk-management-skill.md) to prioritise remediation
- Use the [Consent Manager Skill](skills/dpdp-consent-manager-skill.md) if Domains 2–3 are weak

### 30–59: Weak Compliance

Urgent action required. Establish foundational controls immediately.

- Begin with the [Compliance Roadmap Agent](agents/dpdp-compliance-roadmap-agent.md)
- Use the [Master DPDP Agent](agents/meity-dpdp-privacy-agent.md) to set up DPO and policies
- Use the [Breach Notification Agent](agents/dpdp-breach-notification-agent.md) to build incident response capability
- Use the [Training & Awareness Skill](skills/dpdp-training-awareness-skill.md) for staff readiness

### 0–29: Critical

Immediate programme needed. Engage legal counsel and begin from the [Decision Tree](DECISION_TREE.md).

- Start with the [Decision Tree](DECISION_TREE.md) to identify applicable obligations
- Use the [Compliance Roadmap Agent](agents/dpdp-compliance-roadmap-agent.md) for an emergency implementation plan
- Prioritise the [Incident Response Skill](skills/dpdp-incident-response-skill.md) and [Breach Notification Agent](agents/dpdp-breach-notification-agent.md)
- Use the [Privacy Risk Management Skill](skills/dpdp-privacy-risk-management-skill.md) to assess processor risks
